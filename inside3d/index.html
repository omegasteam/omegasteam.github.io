What is Inside3D?

Inside3D by OmegaSTEAM is a unique service that transforms 3D designs into immersive virtual reality experiences. Students and creators can submit their 3D models in formats such as OBJ or FBX, and our team converts them into interactive VR applications that allow users to walk inside and explore their own designs.

This program is perfect for school projects, creative models, and architectural ideas.

Inside3D – How It Works

Create your dream home or any 3D model using Tinkercad or other 3D software.

Export the design as an OBJ file (recommended). Include textures in a ZIP if needed.

Upload your model through the Inside3D registration page on the OmegaSTEAM website.

Your model will be reviewed to ensure it works correctly for VR conversion.

If approved, complete payment. If you ordered the VR headset, it will be shipped.

Within 3–5 days your model will be converted into a VR experience.

You will receive an APK file to install on your Android phone.

Place your phone in the VR headset and walk inside the world you designed.

Basic Terms & Conditions

VR Experience Creation Service

  1. Service Description
    The service converts a customer-provided 3D model (OBJ/FBX or similar) into an Android VR experience application that allows the user to explore the model using a mobile phone and VR headset.
  2. File Requirements
    Customers must provide a complete and working 3D model, including textures and materials. If files are incomplete, corrupted, or incompatible, additional charges may apply.
  3. Delivery Format
    The final product will be delivered as an Android APK installation file or a secure download link.
  4. Device Compatibility
    The VR app is designed for Android smartphones with gyroscope support and a VR headset (such as Google Cardboard style). Performance may vary depending on device specifications.
  5. Payment Terms
    50% advance payment before starting the project. 50% balance payment before final delivery.
  6. Revisions
    The service includes one round of minor revisions (such as lighting or small adjustments). Major changes or new models will be charged separately.
  7. Processing Time
    Typical delivery time is 2–5 working days depending on model complexity.
  8. Client Responsibility
    The client confirms that they own the rights to the 3D model and that it does not violate copyright or intellectual property laws.
  9. Refund Policy
    Due to the digital nature of the product, payments are non-refundable once development has started.
  10. Support
    Basic installation guidance will be provided. Long-term technical support or future updates may involve additional charges.
  11. Portfolio Use
    The developer may use screenshots or short clips of the project for portfolio or demonstration purposes, unless the client specifically requests privacy.
